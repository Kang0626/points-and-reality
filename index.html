Paradigm Shift

Why 3D Gaussian Splatting?

How 3D Gaussian Splatting replaces heavy polygon meshes with photorealistic radiance fields.

Traditional Photogrammetry

Heavy Polygon Meshes & Jagged Edges

Requires aggressive decimation, causing blurry textures, jagged silhouettes, and missing thin details (wires, fences, glass).

Points & Reality 3DGS

Millions of Sub-Millimeter Splats

Every geometric detail is represented by continuous Gaussian ellipsoids, preserving exact reflections, transparencies, and microscopic curves.

Traditional Photogrammetry

Software Installation & Latency

Clients must install 3D engines, CAD tools, or endure 30+ second loading screens over heavy GB asset downloads.

Points & Reality 3DGS

Instant 60+ FPS Browser WebGL

Self-contained lightweight HTML5 packages run smoothly at 60 FPS on any modern smartphone, tablet, or PC with zero installation.
