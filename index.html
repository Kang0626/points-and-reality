FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How does 3D Gaussian Splatting differ from traditional 3D polygon meshes? +
Traditional 3D meshes wrap 2D textures onto geometric polygon vertices, struggling with reflections, transparency, and organic curves. 3D Gaussian Splatting reconstructs optical radiance using millions of 3D Gaussian ellipsoids, capturing true specular highlights, glass refractions, and sub-millimeter precision in real-time.
Do website visitors need to install any apps or plugins? +
No plugins or apps are required. The Points & Reality viewer is compiled into pure WebGL shaders that run smoothly at 60+ FPS in Safari, Chrome, Edge, and iOS/Android mobile browsers upon clicking the link.
How long does optical capture and 3D reconstruction take? +
Capture takes approximately 3 minutes using any standard smartphone camera. Once footage is uploaded to our neural pipeline, the production-ready 3D digital twin is reconstructed in approximately 1 hour.
How do I integrate this into Shopify or a custom website? +
Simply paste a single line of HTML iframe or React/Liquid component code into your product page template. Responsive mobile layouts and touch gestures are supported out of the box.
